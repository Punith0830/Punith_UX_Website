Mobile product case study · Malaysia

Jobs in your pocket.

Transforming an established employment web portal into an accessible mobile experience for discovering opportunities, applying with confidence and managing a complete job-search journey.

Mobile UX/UI Job search End-to-end flow

Project overview

From web access to mobile momentum.

MYFutureJobs already supported job seekers through a web portal. The mobile product extended that service into a faster, more accessible experience designed around everyday job-search behavior.

01 / Challenge

Desktop dependence limited access.

The existing portal was functional, but navigating it on smaller screens created friction for job seekers who needed to search, respond and apply while on the move.

02 / Objective

Make the core journey feel effortless.

The application needed to simplify job discovery, application tracking, profile management and real-time alerts without losing the capabilities users already relied on.

03 / Direction

Mobile-first, familiar and feasible.

Design decisions balanced user needs, client expectations, business goals and technical constraints so the proposed experience could move cleanly into development.

Find the right opportunity, act on it and stay informed—wherever the search happens.

Design process

A structured path from context to interface.

The work moved through collaborative discovery, technical validation and iterative visual exploration before the end-to-end mobile experience was defined.

01

Understand scope

Aligned the product and design teams on objectives, constraints and the biggest issues in the web experience.

02

Consult the client

Explored expectations, user demographics, critical features and business goals such as retention and completed applications.

03

Check feasibility

Worked with development to prioritize valuable features while accounting for technical limitations and the existing stack.

04

Explore themes

Developed several visual directions across colour, typography, layout and iconography, then combined the strongest elements.

05

Design and test

Built the key journeys and reviewed usability internally to refine navigation, comprehension and task flow.

Visual system

A reusable language for consistency.

The interface uses Roboto, a focused five-colour palette and reusable mobile patterns so every touchpoint feels recognisable while remaining easy to build and scale.

Entry experience

A clear start for every job seeker.

The introduction establishes the national employment service, communicates the platform’s value and gives users a direct choice between exploring jobs and signing in.

Core product

One connected job-search journey.

Recommendations, profile progress, application activity and recruiter actions are brought into a single mobile dashboard so users can immediately understand what needs attention.

Personalised dashboard

Relevant opportunities, visible at a glance.

The home experience combines profile completion, recent activity, recruiter actions, interview calls and job recommendations based on the user’s information.

Understand the role

Decision-ready job details.

Qualifications, working hours, salary, sector and role information are organised into a scannable view with a clear application action.

Refine the search

Filters that match real preferences.

Users can narrow opportunities by date, state, contract type, hours, sector, education level, salary and job category.

Complete with confidence

A reassuring application finish.

A focused success state confirms submission and keeps momentum by offering the next relevant action instead of leaving users at a dead end.

Activity history

A transparent record of progress.

The activity log records applications, profile updates, saved jobs, recruiter messages and notification responses. A chronological view gives job seekers more control over a process that can otherwise feel fragmented.

Professional profile

Better information creates better matches.

The profile experience makes completion visible and divides professional information into manageable sections. Users can update the details that shape recruiter visibility and recommendation quality.

  • Profile progress and completion cues
  • Job preferences and career objectives
  • Work experience, education and skills
  • Downloadable professional profile

What the process reinforced

Designed through alignment, not assumption.

The project demonstrates how stakeholder context, technical feasibility and repeated design review can work together to shape a coherent mobile product.

01

User-centred

Real needs and existing pain points guided the experience rather than treating mobile as a smaller copy of the web portal.

02

Collaborative

Client, product, design and development discussions kept user value, business needs and feasibility aligned.

03

Iterative

Multiple visual directions and feedback-led refinements produced a balanced interface with a consistent system.

04

Connected

Search, application, tracking and profile tasks form one journey instead of isolated features.

Expected outcome

A more accessible and convenient service for Malaysian job seekers—supporting faster discovery, simpler applications, clearer progress and stronger connections with employers.
