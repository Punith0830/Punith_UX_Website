Project overview

One system for a fragmented ESG landscape.

Developed in collaboration with KPMG Singapore, the platform brings sustainability reporting, compliance tracking, benchmarking and target setting into a structured experience tailored to regional and global requirements.

01 / Context

ESG compliance is becoming business-critical.

Organizations need a dependable way to understand performance while responding to evolving sustainability expectations in Singapore and worldwide.

02 / Challenge

Data and reporting lived in disconnected processes.

Manual tracking, fragmented information and complex standards made it difficult for teams to see gaps, compare performance and act confidently.

03 / Direction

Turn compliance into guided progress.

The solution combines clear dashboards, structured assessments and actionable targets so sustainability teams can move from reporting to improvement.

Problem statement

Complex standards. Limited visibility. Too much manual work.

Businesses needed an integrated platform for annual waste and emissions tracking, ESG maturity assessment, GRI and ISSB alignment, peer benchmarking and sustainability target management.

01

Centralize sustainability data and make performance visible.

02

Automate maturity assessments and surface actionable recommendations.

03

Track compliance across GRI, ISSB and local requirements.

04

Support peer benchmarking and measurable target setting.

Research

Evidence shaped the product priorities.

Interviews, observation of existing ESG tools, regulatory analysis and a survey of more than 200 sustainability professionals exposed the areas where teams needed the most support.

83%

found waste-level tracking time-consuming.

76%

needed automated local compliance tracking.

89%

preferred visual dashboards for rapid insight.

68%

wanted Singapore-focused peer benchmarking.

Qualitative

Understand the work behind the reports.

Interviews with sustainability managers, compliance officers and corporate leaders revealed pain points in data collection, reporting workflows and existing tools.

Quantitative

Measure recurring challenges at scale.

Survey responses and adoption feedback helped prioritize automated tracking, interactive dashboards and market-relevant benchmarking.

User journey

User Journey Map

This user journey map highlights the stages Emily goes through in her ESG compliance and sustainability reporting process, her emotions at each stage, and potential design opportunities to enhance her experience with the KPMG Singapore ESG Sustainability Platform.

Scenario

Emily Tan, an ESG Compliance Manager at a Singapore-based corporation, is responsible for ensuring her company meets Singapore’s sustainability regulations. She struggles with fragmented ESG data, manual tracking, and benchmarking ESG performance against competitors. She is searching for a solution that can streamline ESG data collection, compliance tracking, and reporting.

Expectation

Emily expects an intuitive platform for seamless ESG data management and compliance with Singaporean and global regulations. She seeks automated tracking, AI-driven insights, and benchmarking tools. A smooth onboarding experience with guided tutorials is essential. Customizable reporting features should generate accurate and visually engaging sustainability reports.

Stage

Awareness

Action

Identifying ESG compliance challenges and researching sustainability solutions.

Emotion

Overwhelmed by the complexity of sustainability frameworks and regulatory requirements.

Opportunity

Provide educational resources, whitepapers, and webinars on ESG compliance. Offer a free checklist for ESG readiness.

Stage

Consideration

Action

Comparing different ESG platforms and consulting industry peers for recommendations.

Emotion

Cautiously optimistic but skeptical about effectiveness.

Opportunity

Offer live demos, case studies, and free trial access. Provide a side-by-side comparison of competitors.

Stage

Adoption

Action

Signing up, onboarding the ESG platform, and entering sustainability data.

Emotion

Hopeful but concerned about ease of integration.

Opportunity

Stage

Engagement

Action

Regularly tracking ESG metrics, conducting maturity assessments, and generating compliance reports.

Emotion

Confident in the platform but occasionally frustrated with complex analytics.

Opportunity

Stage

Benchmarking & Optimization

Action

Design strategy

Six principles guided the experience.

The design direction balanced usability, regulatory precision, data integration and long-term adaptability.

01

User-centred clarity

Simplify navigation, terminology and reporting tasks for sustainability and compliance teams.

02

Regulatory alignment

Structure workflows around GRI, ISSB, MAS and evolving Singapore requirements.

03

Connected data

Consolidate information across departments and reduce repetitive manual tracking.

04

Actionable benchmarking

Turn industry comparisons into understandable performance priorities.

05

Guided adoption

Support onboarding through clear modules, contextual guidance and training.

06

Future readiness

Make the system flexible enough to adapt to new standards and integrations.

Visual system

Designed for clarity at enterprise scale.

The product uses Arial and KPMG Singapore’s brand palette, supported by predictable navigation, accessible form states and consistent action patterns.

Solution walkthrough

A connected ESG operating view.

Each module addresses a distinct reporting or decision-making need while maintaining a common navigation and interaction model.

Secure entry

A focused start to the reporting experience.

The login experience provides direct access to a complex enterprise product without introducing unnecessary steps.

Emissions at a glance.

The main dashboard combines carbon totals, targets, actuals and monthly trends. Financial-period controls and downloadable reports support recurring analysis.

Water management

Consumption, treatment performance, targets and trends are consolidated into a single environmental view.

Waste management

Annual waste levels become easier to monitor, compare and communicate across reporting periods.

Maturity assessment

Progress made measurable.

Organizations assess environmental, social and governance goals through structured questions and receive a maturity classification from Absent to Adaptive.

Recommendations for the next level.

Assessment results reveal current maturity and provide tailored guidance to help teams strengthen their sustainability practices.

Compliance and targets

Standards translated into guided workflows.

The platform helps teams evaluate disclosure readiness, understand gaps and manage sustainability targets without navigating disconnected tools.

GRI standards

Track reporting progress with context.

Questionnaires, status labels, completion indicators and disclosure groups organize a large global framework into a manageable workflow.

ISSB assessment

Identify disclosure gaps before reporting.

The assessment categorizes requirements across governance, strategy, risk, metrics and emissions to expose areas that need attention.
