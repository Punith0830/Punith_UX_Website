Mobile product case study · Campus services

Laundry, made clear.

A smarter, simpler laundry experience for college life—redesigned around transparent wash limits, clear status updates and efficient multi-role operations.

UX auditUX strategyUI redesignMulti-role experience

Project overview

Service meets campus life.

Chota Dhobi partners with college campuses to provide students with a structured laundry service included in their college package. The application serves students, laundry operations teams and platform management.

Industry

B2C campus laundry

A college-based service connecting daily student needs with operational fulfilment.

Platform

Mobile application

A high-frequency product used to request, track and administer laundry services.

Users

Three distinct roles

Students, Admin teams and Super Admin management each require focused permissions and flows.

The old experience made routine laundry feel unnecessarily complicated.

UX audit & key insights

Friction across every role.

The audit reviewed task flows, navigation, hierarchy, interaction consistency and visual clarity across students, Admins and Super Admins.

01

Core actions required too many steps and unnecessary clicks.

02

Remaining washes and package details were hidden or unclear.

03

Inconsistent navigation and UI patterns increased cognitive load.

04

Admin workflows were inefficient and repetitive.

05

Super Admin controls were scattered without a centralized overview.

06

The outdated visual design lacked Dark Mode for frequent use.

Critical UX input

Rules users can understand.

The business model relies on allocated laundry packages. The redesigned experience makes limits, status and permitted actions explicit rather than leaving users to guess.

01

Transparency

Clearly communicates laundry limits, order status and remaining wash counts at the point of need.

02

Usage tracking

Shows how many washes have been used and how many remain in the active package.

03

Operational clarity

Structures Admin and Super Admin information around role-specific daily decisions.

04

Misuse prevention

Uses clear system rules and indicators to guide users through allowed actions only.

User segmentation

One system. Three perspectives.

Each role received dedicated flows, dashboards and permissions so people only interact with information and controls relevant to their responsibility.

01

Students

Goals

  • Request laundry quickly
  • View remaining wash balance
  • Track laundry status
  • Avoid exceeding limits

Pain points

  • Confusing flows
  • Unclear wash-count visibility
02

Admin

Goals

  • Manage daily requests
  • Update order statuses
  • Track usage per user
  • Handle exceptions

Pain points

  • Too many routine steps
  • Poor active-order visibility
03

Super Admin

Goals

  • Define laundry packages
  • Manage users and Admins
  • Monitor colleges
  • Maintain system control

Pain points

  • Scattered controls
  • No centralized overview

UX strategy

Optimize the repeated moments.

The role-based strategy simplified the highest-frequency tasks, removed redundant screens and elevated wash-count visibility into a primary interface element.

01

Separate role journeys

Dedicated flows and permissions for students, Admins and Super Admins.

02

Shorten core tasks

Student laundry requests became a short, guided sequence.

03

Support operations

Admin actions were optimized for speed and repeated daily use.

04

Centralize control

Super Admin experiences focused on overview, package assignment and system management.

Low-fidelity exploration

Structure before polish.

The wireframes established a consistent task sequence from account access through wash initiation, status tracking, history and profile management.

Typography & color

Designed for quick reading.

Roboto was selected for consistent legibility across student, Admin and Super Admin screens. The palette supports a clear hierarchy in both light and dark modes.
